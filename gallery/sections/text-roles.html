Text roles

Screen heading

.ds-lede: the one sentence right under a heading that says what this screen is for.

A plain paragraph needs no class. It is body text at the base size and the normal colour, for any sentence that explains, describes or instructs.

.ds-note: small print. A footnote, an aside, or the line beside a price.

.ds-help: a hint attached to this field. It turns red when the field is invalid.
